Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1.What is MPLS?
MPLS (Multiprotocol Label Switching) is a routing technique used in telecommunications networks to direct data packets along predefined paths. It provides faster and more efficient data forwarding by using labels to switch packets instead of traditional IP routing.
2.What are the prerequisites for Cisco MPLS certification?
There are no specific prerequisites for Cisco MPLS certification. However, having a basic understanding of networking concepts and experience with Cisco routers and switches will be beneficial.
3.How long does it take to complete the Cisco MPLS certification training?
The duration of the training can vary based on the specific training program you choose. It typically ranges from a few days to several weeks, depending on the depth of coverage and the training schedule.
4.What is the format of the Cisco MPLS certification exam?
The Cisco MPLS certification exam consists of multiple-choice and simulation questions. It assesses your knowledge and skills in MPLS technology, including MPLS fundamentals, MPLS VPNs, MPLS Traffic Engineering, and MPLS troubleshooting.
5.Can I retake the Cisco MPLS certification exam if I don't pass on the first attempt?
Yes, if you don't pass the exam on your first attempt, you can retake it. Cisco allows candidates to retake the exam after a waiting period. It is recommended to use the waiting period to further review and strengthen your understanding of MPLS concepts.
