CompTIA CASP Certification Process:
Exam Requirements: The CompTIA CASP certification requires passing a single exam (CAS-004). This exam evaluates your knowledge and skills in various cybersecurity domains, including risk management, enterprise security operations, research and collaboration, and integration of enterprise security.
Exam Format: The CompTIA CASP exam consists of multiple-choice and performance-based questions. The exam duration is approximately 165 minutes, and a passing score is required to obtain the certification.
Certification Renewal: The CompTIA CASP certification is valid for three years. To maintain your certification, you can participate in continuing education activities, such as attending relevant training, participating in industry events, or earning higher-level CompTIA certifications.
