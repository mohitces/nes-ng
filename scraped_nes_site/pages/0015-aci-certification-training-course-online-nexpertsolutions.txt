Implementing Cisco Application Centric Infrastructure v1.0 (300-620)
Exam Description:
Implementing Cisco Application Centric Infrastructure v1.0 (DCACI 300-620) is a 90- minute exam that is associated with the CCNP Data Center Certification. This exam tests a candidate's knowledge of Cisco switches in ACI mode including configuration, implementation, and management.
20% 1.0 ACI Fabric Infrastructure
1.1 Describe ACI topology and hardware
1.2 Describe ACI Object Model
1.3 Utilize faults, event record, and audit log
1.4 Describe ACI fabric discovery
1.5 Implement ACI policies
1.6 Implement ACI logical constructs
15% 2.0 ACI Packet Forwarding
2.1 Describe endpoint learning
2.2 Implement bridge domain configuration knob (unicast routing, Layer 2 unknown hardware proxy, ARP flooding)
20% 3.0 External Network Connectivity
3.1 Implement Layer 2 out (STP/MCP basics)
3.2 Implement Layer 3 out (excludes transit routing and VRF route leaking)
15% 4.0 Integrations
4.1 Implement VMware vCenter DVS integration
4.2 Describe resolution immediacy in VMM
4.3 Implement service graph (managed and unmanaged)
20% 5.0 ACI Management
5.1 Implement out-of-band and in-band
5.2 Utilize syslog and snmp services
5.3 Implement configuration backup (snapshot/config import export)
5.4 Implement AAA and RBAC
5.5 Configure an upgrade
10% 6.0 ACI Anywhere
6.1 Describe multipod
6.2 Describe multisite
FAQ
1. What is Cisco ACI Certification Training?
Cisco ACI Certification Training is a program designed to teach individuals how to deploy, manage, and troubleshoot Cisco's Application Centric Infrastructure (ACI) solution. It is a comprehensive training that covers all aspects of Cisco ACI, including policy-driven application deployment, automation, and management.
2. Who is the target audience for Cisco ACI Certification Training?
The target audience for Cisco ACI Certification Training includes network architects, network engineers, data center engineers, and IT professionals who are responsible for designing, deploying, and managing data center networks.
3. What prerequisites are required for this course?
To Enroll in the Cisco ACI Certification Training, you should have a basic understanding of networking concepts and protocols. Familiarity with Cisco's routing and switching technologies is also beneficial.
4. What are the benefits of getting Cisco ACI Certified?
The Cisco ACI Certification demonstrates your proficiency in deploying, managing, and troubleshooting Cisco's ACI solution. It validates your knowledge of advanced networking concepts and helps you gain credibility in the industry. Additionally, it can enhance your career prospects and earning potential.
5. How can I prepare myself before attending the Cisco ACI Certification Training?
Before attending the Cisco ACI Certification Training, you can familiarize yourself with the ACI solution by reading Cisco's ACI documentation and watching online tutorials. You can also practice using the ACI simulator or virtual lab environment.
6. What topics are covered in the Cisco ACI Certification Training?
The Cisco ACI Certification Training covers the following topics:
• Introduction to ACI
• ACI Architecture and Components
• ACI Fabric Operation and Management
• ACI Policy and Automation
• ACI Integration with Other Cisco Technologies
• ACI Troubleshooting Techniques
7. What are the hands-on lab activities included in the course?
The Cisco ACI Certification Training includes hands-on lab activities that allow you to practice configuring and managing an ACI fabric. The labs cover topics such as creating tenants, application profiles, contracts, and policies.
8. What kind of support is available to students during the course?
During the course, students have access to an instructor who can answer their questions and provide guidance. Additionally, students can access online support resources and community forums.
9. What is the duration of the Cisco ACI Certification Training?
The duration of the Cisco ACI Certification Training depends on the training provider and the learning format. Typically, it takes around 40-50 Hours to complete the training.
10. What is the format of the Cisco ACI Certification exam?
The Cisco ACI Certification exam is a 90-minute test consisting of 60-70 multiple-choice and simulation questions.
11. What kind of job roles can I get after completing Cisco ACI Certification Training?
After completing the Cisco ACI Certification Training, you can apply for job roles such as Network Engineer, Network Administrator, Network Consultant, and Data Center Engineer.
12. Is there any post-course support or resources available to help me prepare for the exam?
Yes, we provide online resources and study materials to help you prepare for the Cisco ACI Certification exam. Additionally, some training providers offer exam preparation courses.
13. How long is the Cisco ACI Certification valid for?
The Cisco ACI Certification is valid for three years. we provide online resources and study materials to help you prepare for the Cisco ACI Certification exam. Additionally, some training providers offer exam preparation courses.
14. Can I recertify my Cisco ACI Certification?
Yes, you can recertify your Cisco ACI Certification by passing a relevant exam or earning Continuing Education credits.
15. What is the process for recertification?
To recertify your Cisco ACI Certification, you can take and pass a relevant exam, such as a higher-level certification exam or a technology-focused exam. Alternatively, you can earn Continuing Education credits by attending Cisco-approved training or events.
