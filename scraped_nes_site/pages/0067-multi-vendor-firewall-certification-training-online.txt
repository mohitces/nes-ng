Overview
Get hands-on experience with the latest firewall technologies from Cisco, Check Point, and Palo Alto.
Welcome to our new course - Multi Vendor Firewall. In this course, we offer 100% job guarantee to our students. Our course covers various types of firewalls from different vendors, including Cisco Firepower, CCSA, CCSE, and Palo Alto.
With this course, you will get hands-on training on configuring and managing firewall devices. Our course is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of firewall technologies and their implementation. The course will be taught by certified experts with years of experience in the industry.
Included Courses:
Our Multi Vendor Firewall course includes the following courses:
Cisco Firepower:
Cisco Firepower is a unified next-generation firewall platform that provides advanced threat protection and security analytics. In this course, students will learn how to configure, deploy and manage Cisco Firepower firewall.
Check Point Certified Security Administrator (CCSA):
Check Point CCSA certification is designed for security administrators who want to manage and configure Check Point Security solutions. In this course, students will learn how to configure, deploy and manage Check Point firewalls.
Check Point Certified Security Expert (CCSE):
Check Point CCSE certification is designed for security experts who want to manage and configure advanced Check Point Security solutions. In this course, students will learn how to configure, deploy and manage advanced Check Point firewalls.
Palo Alto Networks Firewall:
Palo Alto Networks Firewall is a next-generation firewall that provides advanced threat protection and network security. In this course, students will learn how to configure, deploy and manage Palo Alto firewalls.
We are committed to providing our students with the best training and career opportunities. Upon completion of our Multi Vendor Firewall course, we offer 100% job guarantee to our students. Our placement team works closely with leading companies in the cybersecurity industry to ensure that our students get the best job opportunities.
Enroll now in our Multi Vendor Firewall course and take the first step towards a successful career in the cybersecurity industry. Our experienced trainers will provide you with hands-on training and practical experience to prepare you for the challenges of the real-world.
Course Duration:
The Multi-Vendor Firewall course is a 7 to 8 months program that covers all the above-mentioned courses. The course is designed to provide students with in-depth knowledge of firewall technologies and their practical applications.
