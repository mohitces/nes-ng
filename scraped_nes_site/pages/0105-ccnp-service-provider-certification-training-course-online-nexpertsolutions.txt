Implementing and Operating Cisco Service Provider Network Core Technologies v1.0 (350-501)
Exam Description:
Implementing and Operating Cisco Service Provider Network Core Technologies v1.0 (SPCOR 350-501) is a 120-minute exam associated with the CCNP and CCIE Service Provider Certifications. This exam tests a candidate's knowledge of implementing core service provider network technologies including core architecture, services, networking, automation, quality of services, security, and network assurance. The course, Implementing and Operating Cisco Service Provider Network Core Technologies, helps candidates to prepare for this exam.

1.4 Describe QoS architecture

1. What is CCNP Service Provider Training?
· CCNP Service Provider (Cisco Certified Network Professional Service Provider) Training is a certification program offered by Cisco Systems that provides advanced knowledge and skills to IT professionals to design, implement, and troubleshoot service provider networks. This program covers a range of topics such as advanced routing protocols, Quality of Service (QoS), Multiprotocol Label Switching (MPLS), and Virtual Private Networks (VPNs), among others.
· The CCNP Service Provider certification is intended for professionals who work in service provider organizations, including network engineers, network technicians, network administrators, and support engineers. This certification validates their ability to manage and operate complex service provider networks and demonstrates their expertise in Cisco technologies and solutions.
· By completing the CCNP Service Provider Training, IT professionals can enhance their career prospects and gain recognition for their skills and expertise. The training includes both theoretical and hands-on practical components, enabling candidates to gain a deep understanding of service provider networks and prepare them for real-world challenges.

2. What are the prerequisites for CCNP Service Provider Training?
To Enroll in CCNP Service Provider (Cisco Certified Network Professional Service Provider) Training, candidates must meet certain prerequisites to ensure they have the necessary knowledge and skills to succeed in the program. The prerequisites for CCNP Service Provider Training include:
1. Valid CCNA Service Provider certification or any CCIE certification.
2. Basic knowledge of IP addressing, routing, and switching.
3. Familiarity with Cisco IOS XR software.
4. Experience working with service provider networks.
5. Understanding of networking protocols, including Border Gateway Protocol (BGP), Multiprotocol Label Switching (MPLS), and Quality of Service (QoS).
6. Knowledge of Virtual Private Network (VPN) technologies and network security.
Candidates who do not have the above prerequisites can take the necessary training courses to gain the required knowledge and skills before enrolling in CCNP Service Provider Training.

3. What topics are covered in CCNP Service Provider Training?
CCNP Service Provider (Cisco Certified Network Professional Service Provider) Training is designed to provide IT professionals with the advanced knowledge and skills required to design, implement, and troubleshoot complex service provider networks. The program covers a wide range of topics related to service provider networking, including:
1. IP routing technologies, including advanced routing protocols such as OSPF, BGP, and IS-IS.
2. MPLS (Multiprotocol Label Switching) technologies, including MPLS Layer 3 VPNs, MPLS Layer 2 VPNs, MPLS Traffic Engineering (TE), and MPLS multicast.
3. Quality of Service (QoS) technologies, including classification, marking, policing, shaping, and congestion management.
4. Service Provider IPv6, including IPv6 addressing and routing, and transition technologies such as Dual Stack and 6PE/6VPE.
5. VPN (Virtual Private Network) technologies, including site-to-site VPNs and remote access VPNs.
6. Network security, including access control, firewall technologies, and intrusion prevention systems.
7. Network management, including network monitoring, device management, and network automation.
The training includes both theoretical and hands-on practical components to ensure that candidates gain a deep understanding of service provider networking concepts and technologies and can apply them to real-world scenarios. By completing CCNP Service Provider Training, IT professionals can demonstrate their expertise in service provider networking and advance their careers in the industry.

10. What is the validity of CCNP Service Provider certification?
The CCNP Service Provider certification is valid for three years from the date of certification. After this period, candidates must recertify to maintain their certification status. Recertification can be achieved by passing any one of the following exams:
1. Pass any current CCNP-level exam or
2. Pass any current 642-XXX Professional-level or Cisco Specialist exam (excluding Sales Specialist exams or Meeting Place Specialist exams, Implementing Cisco Telepresence Installations (ITI) exams, Cisco Leading Virtual Classroom Instruction exams, or any 650 online exams).
Alternatively, candidates can also recertify by passing a current CCIE written exam or CCDE written or practical exam.
It is important to note that recertification is necessary to maintain the validity of the CCNP Service Provider certification. Failing to recertify before the certification expiration date can result in the certification becoming inactive, which can impact job opportunities and career growth.
In summary, the CCNP Service Provider certification is valid for three years, and candidates must recertify to maintain their certification status. Recertification can be achieved by passing certain exams or by passing a current CCIE or CCDE written or practical exam.
