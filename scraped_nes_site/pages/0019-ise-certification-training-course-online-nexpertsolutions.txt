Implementing Cisco Secure Access Solutions (300-208)
Exam Description:
The Implementing Cisco Secure Access Solutions (SISAS) (300-208) exam tests whether a network security engineer knows the components and architecture of secure access, by utilizing 802.1X and Cisco TrustSec. This 90-minute exam consists of 65–75 questions and assesses knowledge of Cisco Identity Services Engine (ISE) architecture, solution, and components as an overall network threat mitigation and endpoint control solutions. It also includes the fundamental concepts of bring your own device (BYOD) using posture and profiling services of ISE. Candidates can prepare for this exam by taking the Implementing Cisco Secure Access Solutions (SISAS) course.

FAQ
1. What is Cisco ISE Certification Training?
Cisco ISE (Identity Services Engine) Certification Training is a program that provides learners with the knowledge and skills required to design, implement, configure, and support Cisco ISE solutions. Cisco ISE is a network access control and policy enforcement platform that allows organizations to secure their networks by controlling user and device access.
2. What are the benefits of getting Cisco ISE certification?
Cisco ISE certification demonstrates that a professional has the knowledge and skills required to design, implement, configure, and support Cisco ISE solutions. The certification can help professionals enhance their career opportunities, increase their earning potential, and gain recognition in the industry.
3. How long is the Cisco ISE training?
The duration of the Cisco ISE training program Is 40-50 Hours.
4. What is the mode of Cisco ISE training - online or offline?
The mode of Cisco ISE training combines both online and in-person learning.
5. What are the prerequisites for the Cisco ISE course?
The prerequisites for the Cisco ISE course participants are expected to have basic knowledge of networking concepts and experience with Cisco devices.
6. What is the fee structure and are there any discounts available?
The fee structure for the Cisco ISE training program can vary depending on the in which time you enroll in this course. Discounts may be available for early registration, group registration, or for certain categories of learners, such as students or veterans.
7. What are the job opportunities after completing the Cisco ISE training?
After completing the Cisco ISE training, professionals can pursue career opportunities such as network engineer, network security engineer, security analyst, security consultant, and security architect.
8. How can I Enroll for Cisco ISE Training Online?
To Enroll for the Cisco ISE course Online you can go Net Expert Solutions Website as well as NES Physical Center.
9. How do I register for the Cisco ISE certification exam?
To register for the Cisco ISE certification exam, you can visit the Cisco certification website, create an account, select the exam you want to take, and pay the exam fee. You can also register for the exam through authorized testing centres Net Expert Solutions is also the Pearson VUE Authorised centre.
