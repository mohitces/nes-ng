FAQ
1. What is Cisco SD Access Certification Training?
Cisco SD Access Certification Training is a program that helps individuals gain expertise in Cisco's Software-Defined Access (SD-Access) solution. It covers the implementation and operation of this solution in enterprise networks.
2. What prerequisites are required for this course?
To Enroll in the Cisco SD Access Certification Training, you should have a basic understanding of networking concepts and protocols. Familiarity with Cisco's routing and switching technologies is also beneficial.
3. What are the benefits of getting Cisco SD Access Certified?
The Cisco SD Access Certification demonstrates your proficiency in implementing and managing Cisco's SD-Access solution. It validates your knowledge of advanced networking concepts and helps you gain credibility in the industry. Additionally, it can enhance your career prospects and earning potential.
4. How long does it take to complete the Cisco SD Access Certification Training?
The duration of the Cisco SD Access Certification Training depends on the training provider and the learning format. Typically, it takes around 40-50 Hours to complete the training.
5. What topics are covered in the Cisco SD Access Certification Training?
The Cisco SD Access Certification Training covers the following topics: • Introduction to Software-Defined Access • SD-Access Architecture and Components • SD-Access Fabric Control Plane and Data Plane • Integration with Other Cisco Technologies • Policy-Based Segmentation and Automation • Advanced Troubleshooting Techniques
6. What kind of job roles can I get after completing Cisco SD Access Certification Training?
After completing the Cisco SD Access Certification Training, you can apply for job roles such as Network Engineer, Network Administrator, Network Consultant, and Network Architect.
7. Is there any exam I need to pass to become certified?
Yes, you need to pass the Cisco Certified Specialist - Enterprise SD-WAN Implementation exam (300-415 ENSDWI) to become Cisco SD Access certified.
8. What is the format of the Cisco SD Access Certification exam?
The Cisco SD Access Certification exam is a 90-minute test consisting of 60-70 multiple-choice and simulation questions.
9.What is the validity period of the Cisco SD Access Certification?
The Cisco SD Access Certification is valid for three years. After that, you need to recertify by passing a relevant exam or earning Continuing Education credits.
