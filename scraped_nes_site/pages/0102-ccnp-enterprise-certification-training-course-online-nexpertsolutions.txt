Implementing Cisco Enterprise Network Core Technologies v1.0 (350-401)
Exam Description:
Implementing Cisco Enterprise Network Core Technologies v1.0 (ENCOR 350-401) is a 120-minute exam associated with the CCNP and CCIE Enterprise Certifications. This exam tests a candidate's knowledge of implementing core enterprise network technologies including dual stack (IPv4 and IPv6) architecture, virtualization, infrastructure, network assurance, security and automation. The course, Implementing Cisco Enterprise Network Core Technologies, helps candidates to prepare for this exam.
15% 1.0 Architecture
1.1 Explain the different design principles used in an enterprise network
1.2 Analyze design principles of a WLAN deployment
1.3 Differentiate between on-premises and cloud infrastructure deployments
1.4 Explain the working principles of the Cisco SD-WAN solution
1.5 Explain the working principles of the Cisco SD-Access solution
1.6 Describe concepts of wired and wireless QoS
1.7 Differentiate hardware and software switching mechanisms
10% 2.0 Virtualization
2.1 Describe device virtualization technologies

FAQ
1.What is CCNP Enterprise and What are the Benefits of This Certification?
CCNP Enterprise is a certification program offered by Cisco that focuses on enterprise networking solutions. This certification is designed for professionals who want to advance their careers in the field of networking. The benefits of CCNP Enterprise certification include increased job opportunities, higher salaries, and recognition as an expert in the field of enterprise networking.
2. What are the Prerequisites for Enrolling in a CCNP Enterprise Course?
To enroll in a CCNP Enterprise course, you must have a valid CCNA certification or any Cisco Certified Internetwork Expert (CCIE) certification. You should also have a good understanding of networking concepts and protocols.
3. How Long Does It Take to Complete a CCNP Enterprise Course?
The duration of a CCNP Enterprise course varies depending on the training provider and the mode of study. Generally, it takes about 6-12 months to complete a CCNP Enterprise course.
4. What Topics are Covered in a CCNP Enterprise Course?
A CCNP Enterprise course covers a wide range of topics, including network design, routing protocols, switching, wireless networking, and security. The course also includes hands-on lab exercises to help you gain practical experience.
5. What Study Materials are Provided in a CCNP Enterprise Course?
CCNP Enterprise courses provide study materials such as textbooks, lab manuals, and online resources. You can also access practice exams and quizzes to help you prepare for the certification exams.
6. How Can I Best Prepare for CCNP Enterprise Exams During the Course?
To prepare for CCNP Enterprise exams, you should attend all classes, complete all lab exercises, and review all study materials. You can also join study groups and participate in online forums to discuss concepts and ask questions.
7. What is the Format of CCNP Enterprise Exams and How are They Scored?
CCNP Enterprise exams are computer-based and consist of multiple-choice questions, simulations, and drag-and-drop questions. The exams are scored on a scale of 300-1000, and you must score at least 825 to pass.
8. What Additional Resources are Available to Help Me Understand CCNP Enterprise Concepts?
There are many resources available to help you understand CCNP Enterprise concepts, including online forums, study groups, and Cisco's official website. You can also access online training courses and attend webinars to gain more knowledge
9. How Often are CCNP Enterprise Course Materials Updated?
CCNP Enterprise course materials are updated regularly to reflect changes in technology and industry standards. You should check with your training provider to ensure that you have the most up-to-date study materials.
10. Can I Get Additional Help or Support if I Struggle with CCNP Enterprise Concepts?
Yes, you can get additional help or support if you struggle with CCNP Enterprise concepts. You can reach out to your instructor, join study groups, or hire a tutor to help you understand difficult concepts.
11. How Can I Use My CCNP Enterprise Certification to Advance My Career?
CCNP Enterprise certification can help you advance your career by opening up new job opportunities and increasing your earning potential. You can also use your certification to demonstrate your expertise in enterprise networking and gain recognition in the industry.
12. What are Some Job Opportunities Available to CCNP Enterprise Certified Professionals?
CCNP Enterprise certified professionals can work in a variety of roles, including network engineer, network administrator, network analyst, and network architect. They can also work in industries such as healthcare, finance, and telecommunications.
13. Can I Continue My Learning and Development After Completing a CCNP Enterprise Course?
Yes, you can continue your learning and development after completing a CCNP Enterprise course. You can pursue advanced certifications such as CCIE or attend training courses to gain more knowledge and skills.
14. How Can I Maintain My CCNP Enterprise Certification and Keep it Current?
To maintain your CCNP Enterprise certification, you must recertify every three years by passing a recertification exam or earning Continuing Education credits. You should also stay up-to-date with industry trends and technology advancements.
15. What are Some Popular Career Paths for CCNP Enterprise Certified Professionals?
CCNP Enterprise certified professionals can pursue a variety of career paths, including network engineering, network architecture, and network security. They can also work in roles such as IT manager, project manager, and consultant.
16. How Much Does a CCNP Enterprise Course Cost?
The cost of a CCNP Enterprise course varies depending on the training provider and the mode of study. Generally, it can cost anywhere from $1,000 to $5,000.
17. Is it Possible to Study for CCNP Enterprise Certification Online?
Yes, it is possible to study for CCNP Enterprise certification online. Many training providers offer online courses and resources to help you prepare for the certification exams.
18. What are the Differences Between CCNP Enterprise and Other Cisco Certifications?
CCNP Enterprise focuses specifically on enterprise networking solutions, while other Cisco certifications may focus on other areas such as security or collaboration. CCNP Enterprise is also a more advanced certification than CCNA.
19. Are There Any Prerequisites for Taking the CCNP Enterprise Exams?
Yes, you must have a valid CCNA certification or any Cisco Certified Internetwork Expert (CCIE) certification to take the CCNP Enterprise exams.
20. What is the Success Rate of CCNP Enterprise Certified Professionals in the Job Market?
CCNP Enterprise certified professionals have a high success rate in the job market, as they are recognized as experts in the field of enterprise networking. However, success also depends on factors such as experience, skills, and industry demand.
21. What are the differences between CCNP Enterprise and other Cisco certifications?
The main differences between CCNP Enterprise and other Cisco certifications are the level of difficulty and the scope of topics covered. CCNP Enterprise is a professional-level certification that focuses on enterprise networking solutions, while other Cisco certifications may focus on specific technologies or areas of expertise.
22. Are there any prerequisites for taking CCNP Enterprise exams?
