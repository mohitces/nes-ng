Implementing Cisco SD-WAN Solutions v1.0 (300-415)
Exam Description:
Implementing Cisco SD-WAN Solutions v1.0 (ENSDWI 300-415) is a 90-minute exam associated with the CCNP Enterprise Certification. This exam certifies a candidate's knowledge of Cisco’s SD-WAN solution including SD-WAN architecture, controller deployment, edge router deployment, policies, security, quality or service, multicast and management and operations. The course, Implementing Cisco SD-WAN Solutions, helps candidates to prepare for this exam.
20% 1.0 Architecture

1.2 Describe WAN Edge platform types, capabilities (vEdges, cEdges)
15% 2.0 Controller Deployment
2.1 Describe controller cloud deployment
2.2 Describe Controller on-Prem Deployment
2.3 Configure and verify certificates and whitelisting
2.4 Troubleshoot control-plane connectivity between controllers

FAQ
1. What is Cisco SD WAN Certification Training?
Cisco SD-WAN (Software-Defined Wide Area Networking) Certification Training is a program that teaches IT professionals how to design, deploy, and troubleshoot Cisco SD-WAN solutions. The training focuses on the Cisco SD-WAN architecture, controller deployment, security, policies, quality of service (QoS), and migration from legacy WAN technologies.
2.Who is the intended audience for Cisco SD WAN Certification Training?
The intended audience for Cisco SD-WAN Certification Training includes network engineers, network administrators, network architects, and IT professionals who are involved in WAN deployments.
3.What are the prerequisites for Cisco SD WAN Certification Training?
There are no formal prerequisites for Cisco SD-WAN Certification Training. However, Cisco recommends that candidates have a basic understanding of networking technologies and concepts.
4.What are the benefits of getting certified in Cisco SD WAN?
Getting certified in Cisco SD-WAN can help IT professionals gain expertise in SD-WAN technologies and demonstrate their proficiency to employers. Certified professionals can also expand their career opportunities and increase their earning potential.
5.How long does Cisco SD WAN Certification Training typically take?
The duration of Cisco SD-WAN Certification Training varies depending on the training provider and the training format chosen. It can range from a few days to several weeks.
6.What topics are covered in Cisco SD WAN Certification Training?
Cisco SD-WAN Certification Training covers a range of topics, including the Cisco SD-WAN architecture, controller deployment, security, policies, quality of service (QoS), and migration from legacy WAN technologies.
7.Is there a certification exam for Cisco SD WAN Certification Training?
Yes, there is a certification exam for Cisco SD-WAN Certification Training. The exam is designed to test the candidate's knowledge and skills related to Cisco SD-WAN technologies.
8.What is the format of the Cisco SD WAN Certification exam?
The Cisco SD-WAN Certification exam is a proctored, computer-based exam that consists of multiple-choice questions. The exam duration is 90 minutes.
9.What kind of job roles can one expect after getting certified in Cisco SD WAN?
After getting certified in Cisco SD-WAN, one can expect to work in roles such as Network Engineer, Network Administrator, Network Architect, and SD-WAN Engineer.
10.What is the cost of Cisco SD WAN Certification Training?
The cost of Cisco SD-WAN Certification Training varies depending on the training provider and the training format chosen. It typically ranges from a few hundred to a few thousand dollars. The cost of the certification exam is additional and varies depending on the location.
