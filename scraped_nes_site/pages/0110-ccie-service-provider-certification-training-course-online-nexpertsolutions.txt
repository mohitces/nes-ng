CCIE Service Provider (v5.0) Exam Topics – Practical Exam
Exam Description:
The Cisco CCIE Service Provider (v5.0) Practical Exam is an eight-hour, hands-on exam that requires a candidate to plan, design, implement, operate, and optimize dual stack solutions (IPv4 and IPv6) of complex Service Provider networks.
1. Core Routing (25%)
1.1 Interior Gateway Protocol
1.2 Border Gateway Protoco
1.3 Multicast
1.4 Multiprotocol Label Switching
1.5 MPLS Traffic Engineering
2. Architectures and Services (25%)
2.1 Virtualized Infrastructure
2.2 Large scale MPLS Architecture
2.3 Carrier Ethernet
2.4 L3VPN
2.5 Internet service
2.6 Multicast VPN
2.7 Quality of Service for Core, Distribution and Access
3. Access Connectivity (10%)
3.1 Layer-2 Connectivity
3.2 Layer-3 Connectivity
4. High Availability and Fast Convergence (10%)
4.1 High Availability

5. Security (10%)

FAQ
1. What is CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification?
CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification is a professional-level certification offered by Cisco Systems, which demonstrates advanced knowledge and skills in planning, designing, implementing, and operating complex service provider networks.
2. What are the eligibility requirements for CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification?
To be eligible for CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification, candidates must pass a written exam and a hands-on lab exam. There are no formal prerequisites for the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification, but candidates should have a strong foundation in networking technologies and several years of relevant work experience.
3.How long does it take to prepare for the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification exam?
The amount of time required to prepare for the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification exam can vary depending on a candidate's prior knowledge and experience. However, most candidates typically spend several months or more preparing for the exam.
4.What topics are covered in the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification training program?
The CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification training program covers a wide range of topics, including advanced routing and switching, service provider VPNs, MPLS, QoS, network security, and network management.
5. Who should Enroll in the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification training program?
The CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification training program is designed for experienced network professionals who are responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining service provider networks.
6.What are the benefits of obtaining CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification?
CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification is widely recognized as a prestigious certification in the networking industry, which can open up new career opportunities and demonstrate advanced knowledge and skills in service provider networking technologies.
7.How difficult is the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification exam?
The CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification exam is one of the most challenging certification exams in the networking industry. It requires a deep understanding of complex networking technologies and a significant amount of hands-on experience.
8.What resources are available to help students prepare for the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification exam?
Cisco provides a variety of resources to help students prepare for the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification exam, including study materials, practice exams, and hands-on lab training.
9.What is the success rate for students who take the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification exam?
The success rate for students who take the CCIE Service Provider v5.0 Certification exam varies from year to year. However, the exam is widely considered to be challenging, and many candidates require multiple attempts to pass.
10.What are the job opportunities for CCIE Service Provider v5.0 certified professionals?
CCIE Service Provider v5.0 certified professionals are in high demand and can find job opportunities in a variety of industries, including telecommunications, cloud service providers, and data center hosting companies. Job titles may include network engineer, network architect, or network operations manager.
