CompTIA Server+ Certification Process:
Exam Requirements: The CompTIA Server+ certification requires passing a single exam (SK0-005). This exam validates your knowledge and skills in various server-related domains, including installation, configuration, maintenance, security, and troubleshooting.
Exam Format: The CompTIA Server+ exam consists of multiple-choice and performance-based questions. The exam duration is approximately 90 minutes, and a passing score is required to obtain the certification.
Certification Renewal: The CompTIA Server+ certification is valid for three years. To maintain your certification, you can participate in continuing education activities, such as attending relevant training or earning higher-level CompTIA certifications.
