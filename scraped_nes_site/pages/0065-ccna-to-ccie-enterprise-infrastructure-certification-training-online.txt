CCNA (Cisco Certified Network Associate)
is an entry-level certification that provides the foundation for networking concepts. The CCNA course covers the basics of networking, such as IP addressing, routing protocols, LAN and WAN technologies, network security, and more. By completing this course, you'll gain a strong understanding of network fundamentals and be prepared to take the CCNA certification exam.
CCNP (Cisco Certified Network Professional)
Enterprise is an intermediate-level certification that focuses on advanced enterprise networking solutions. The CCNP Enterprise course covers topics such as advanced routing, switching, troubleshooting, security, and wireless networking. This course builds on the knowledge gained in the CCNA course and prepares students for the CCNP Enterprise certification exam.
CCIE (Cisco Certified Internetwork Expert)
Enterprise is an expert-level certification that validates the skills and knowledge required to design, implement, and troubleshoot complex enterprise networks. The CCIE Enterprise course covers advanced topics such as network automation, design principles, and network architecture. This course builds on the knowledge gained in the CCNA and CCNP Enterprise courses and prepares students for the CCIE Enterprise certification exam.
